PAGE 3 — HYPNOTHERAPY

What is hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy combines therapeutic techniques with hypnosis to help you make changes to thoughts, feelings, behaviours and habits.

Hypnosis is a naturally occurring state of focused attention and deep relaxation. You may have experienced something similar when you've become completely absorbed in a book, film or journey and temporarily lost awareness of what's happening around you.

During hypnotherapy, this focused state can be used as part of a structured therapeutic process.

And importantly, you remain in control.

---

What does hypnosis feel like?

There's a common misconception that hypnosis means losing control or being unconscious.

It doesn't.

You won't be asleep, and you won't be forced to do anything against your wishes.

Most people remain aware of what is happening throughout a session. You may feel deeply relaxed, focused or pleasantly absorbed.

Everyone experiences hypnosis differently.

[PLACEHOLDER: Short video of Ceri explaining what hypnosis feels like]

---

What happens during a session?

Before hypnosis

We'll talk about what you'd like to achieve and explore anything relevant to your situation.

During hypnosis

I'll guide you through a process designed to help you become relaxed and focused. From there, we'll use therapeutic techniques tailored to your goals.

Afterwards

We'll talk about your experience and any observations or insights you've had.

You can then continue with your day as normal.

---

Will I lose control?

No.

Hypnosis doesn't remove your ability to make decisions or choose what you do.

You can speak, move, open your eyes and stop the process at any time.

The purpose of hypnotherapy is to help you make changes you want to make.

---

Does hypnotherapy work?

Hypnotherapy can be helpful for a range of psychological and behavioural issues, although results vary from person to person.

It isn't a magic cure and it's not appropriate for every problem or every individual.

During your consultation, we'll discuss your circumstances and whether hypnotherapy is a suitable approach for you.

PAGE 7 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will I be asleep during hypnosis?

No. Hypnosis is not the same as sleep. You'll generally remain aware of what's happening and able to communicate throughout the session.

Can anyone be hypnotised?

Most people can experience hypnosis, although the experience varies from person to person. Your willingness to participate and ability to engage with the process are important.

Will I lose control?

No. You remain in control throughout the session and cannot be made to do something you don't want to do.

What if I can't relax?

That's completely okay. You don't need to be exceptionally relaxed or have a particular type of personality to try hypnotherapy.

How quickly will I see results?

Everyone is different. Some people notice changes quickly, while others require more time and practice. We'll discuss realistic expectations during your consultation.

Is hypnotherapy suitable for everyone?

No. Hypnotherapy isn't appropriate for every person or every situation. I'll discuss your circumstances with you and, where necessary, recommend that you seek another form of professional support.
