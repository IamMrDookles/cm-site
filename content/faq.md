faqs:
  - question: "Will I be asleep during hypnosis?"
    answer: "No. Hypnosis is not the same as sleep. You will generally remain aware of what is happening and able to communicate."
  - question: "Can anyone be hypnotised?"
    answer: "Many people can experience hypnosis, although it varies from person to person. A willingness to take part and engage with the process is important. Ceri will discuss suitability with you."
  - question: "Will I lose control?"
    answer: "No. You remain able to choose what you do, communicate and ask to stop. Hypnotherapy is a collaborative process."
  - question: "What if I cannot relax?"
    answer: "That is completely okay. You do not need to reach a perfect state of relaxation or have a particular kind of personality. Ceri will guide you at your own pace."
  - question: "How quickly will I notice a change?"
    answer: "Everyone is different. Some people notice changes sooner than others, and no particular result can be guaranteed. Ceri will discuss realistic expectations and review progress with you."
  - question: "Is hypnotherapy suitable for everyone?"
    answer: "No. It is not appropriate for every person or situation. Ceri will discuss your circumstances and may suggest another kind of professional support where that would be more suitable."
  - question: "Can I drive after hypnotherapy?"
    answer: "Guidance for travel after a session will be confirmed here. Please ask Ceri before booking if driving or another safety-sensitive activity is part of your plans."
  - question: "Is hypnotherapy confidential?"
    answer: "Ceri will explain confidentiality, record keeping and any limits before you begin. A full privacy and confidentiality summary will be published here once confirmed."
  - question: "Do I need to believe in hypnosis for it to work?"
    answer: "You do not need a particular belief about hypnosis. An open mind and willingness to participate are generally more useful than a strong belief either way."
  - question: "Can I stop a session at any time?"
    answer: "Yes. You remain in control and can ask to pause or stop at any point."
