You might recognise the habits or thoughts holding you back and still find yourself repeating them. Hypnotherapy can help you explore those patterns and practise a different response.

My approach is warm, personal and collaborative. As a psychotherapist, I draw on CBT (cognitive behavioural therapy) and psychological hypnotherapy to understand the links between thoughts, feelings and behaviours. I shape each session around what is happening for you, what you would like to change and whether my approach is suitable.
