## What is hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy combines therapeutic techniques with hypnosis. Hypnosis is a state of focused attention that many people experience as relaxation or absorption, a little like becoming immersed in a book, film or familiar journey.

During hypnotherapy, that focused state forms part of a structured therapeutic process. You remain able to choose, communicate and stop at any time.
{{< /section >}}

{{< section tone="sage" width="wide" >}}
{{< split title="What does hypnosis feel like?" label="Video: Ceri explains what hypnosis can feel like" ratio="16 / 10" mediaPosition="right" >}}
Hypnosis is not sleep or a loss of control. Most people remain aware of what is happening and may feel relaxed, focused or pleasantly absorbed.

There is no single correct sensation. Your experience may be different from someone else's, and Ceri will explain the process before you begin.
{{< /split >}}
{{< /section >}}

## What happens during a session?

### Before hypnosis

You will talk about what you would like to achieve and anything relevant to your situation.

### During hypnosis

Ceri will guide you towards a more relaxed, focused state and use techniques selected for your goals.

### Afterwards

You can discuss your experience and any observations you noticed, then continue with your day as usual unless Ceri advises otherwise for your circumstances.
{{< /section >}}

{{< section tone="warm" width="narrow" >}}
## Will I lose control?

No. Hypnosis does not remove your ability to make decisions. You can speak, move, open your eyes or ask to stop at any time. The purpose is to support changes that you want to make.
{{< /section >}}

{{< section tone="default" width="narrow" >}}
## Is hypnotherapy right for everyone?

Hypnotherapy may help with a range of psychological and behavioural concerns, but experiences and outcomes vary. It is not a cure-all and is not appropriate for every person or situation.

Ceri will discuss suitability during your consultation. You can also explore the selected reading on the [resources page](/resources/).
