## Your first step

Deciding to ask for help can be the hardest part. Your initial conversation is a chance for Ceri to understand your situation, for you to ask questions and for both of you to consider whether hypnotherapy is suitable.

You do not need to be especially good at relaxing or switching off. The process will be explained as you go.

## How many sessions will I need?

There is no universal answer. A focused concern may need a small number of sessions, while other goals may benefit from longer support.

Ceri will explain the options once she understands your circumstances and will review progress with you rather than asking you to commit unnecessarily. Exact session structures will be published when confirmed.
